San Francisco publishes 500+ public datasets. Almost none of them get read.

› How it works

Three steps,
every sixty seconds.

01 — MONITOR

Always on, never blinking.

Agents pull SF Open Data, You.com news, Reddit, and X every minute. Trends, backlogs, and headlines get continuous coverage so no slow-burn issue gets missed.

02 — ANALYZE

Connect dots, not list rows.

Gemini interprets each agent's snapshot against past memory, surfaces the underlying story, and rates severity. A coordinator merges related signals across departments.

03 — ACT

One click to a real email.

High-severity findings auto-open GitHub tickets for tracking. For citizens: draft a data-cited email to the right SF official and send it via Gmail, all from the finding.
