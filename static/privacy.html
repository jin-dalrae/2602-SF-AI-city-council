Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-05-14  ·  Effective 2026-05-14
Written to comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California Civil Code § 1798.83 ("Shine the Light"), and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA). California residents have additional rights described in Section 9.

01Who we are

AI City Council ("the dashboard," "we," "us") is an open civic-intelligence tool. The operator of this deployment is the entity hosting it at the URL you accessed; see the contact section for how to reach us.

02What we collect

The dashboard collects only what is needed to operate. There is no analytics tracking, advertising SDK, or third-party fingerprinting.

  • API token — stored in your browser's localStorage on the device you set it on. Transmitted only as an Authorization: Bearer header to this server.
  • Email-draft inputs — your stated name (or "A concerned resident"), your desired outcome, and the selected finding. Forwarded to the AI provider to generate a draft. Not stored on our server beyond the request lifetime.
  • Sent-email payloads — when you press "Send," the recipient address, subject, and body are forwarded to Composio's Gmail integration.
  • Server logs — standard FastAPI/uvicorn access logs (IP, timestamp, request path, status code) on the host.

03Categories of personal information (CCPA § 1798.140)

Mapped to the statutory categories:

  • Identifiers — IP address (server logs). Self-supplied name in email drafts.
  • Internet/Network activity — HTTP request metadata in server logs.
  • Customer records — name + email recipient(s) when you draft or send a message.
  • Sensitive personal information — none collected.
  • Biometric, geolocation, audio/visual, employment, education, financial, inferences — none collected.

04Sources, purposes, retention

Sources: directly from you (form inputs), automatically from your browser (HTTP metadata).

Purposes: operating the dashboard, authenticating mutating actions, generating email drafts, sending civic correspondence at your direction, debugging.

Retention:

  • Email-draft inputs: not retained beyond request lifetime.
  • Sent emails: retained by the recipient and the Gmail account used; we do not store a server-side copy.
  • Server access logs: rotated by the host OS (typically 14–90 days; varies by deployment).
  • Agent findings + embeddings: indefinite, but contain no visitor PII (only AI summaries of public city data).

05What the server stores on disk

Persisted to backend/data/:

  • findings.json — current snapshot of agent findings.
  • findings_history.jsonl — append-only history (tail-trimmed).
  • agent_memory.jsonl — short text summaries plus embedding vectors used for retrieval-augmented analysis.

None of these contain visitor PII. They contain AI-generated summaries of public civic data.

06Sale or sharing of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Therefore, no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is required — but if you would like a written confirmation of non-sale, contact us.

07Service providers

  • Google Gemini — prompts include public dataset summaries and (for email drafts) your inputs. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • You.com — search queries about SF civic topics.
  • Composio — when you create a GitHub issue or send a Gmail message, the action body is forwarded to Composio's API, which proxies to GitHub / Gmail. Subject to Composio's legal terms.
  • SF Open Data (Socrata) — read-only public dataset queries.

These vendors process data on our behalf as "service providers" or "contractors" under the CCPA. They are contractually restricted from using your information for their own marketing or from selling it.

08Cookies & tracking

The dashboard does not set HTTP cookies and does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because it does not track. The only client-side state is your API token in localStorage, which you can clear at any time by clicking the 🔑 button and submitting an empty value, or by clearing site data in your browser.

09Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA (as amended by the CPRA) gives you the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

  • Right to know — what categories of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties we share it with.
  • Right to access — a copy of the specific pieces of personal information we have about you.
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Right to correct — request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing — N/A; we do not sell or share for behavioral advertising.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information — N/A; we do not collect sensitive PI.
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not deny service, charge a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised a CCPA right.

How to exercise: email the operator listed in Section 13. We will respond within 45 days (with a possible 45-day extension as permitted by law). We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

Authorized agents: you may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. The agent must provide written, signed authorization, and we may require you to confirm directly.

Shine the Light (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83): California residents may request a list of personal information disclosed to third parties for those third parties' direct marketing purposes during the preceding calendar year. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing.

10Children's privacy

The dashboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA). For California residents under 16, we do not sell or share personal information; affirmative opt-in would otherwise be required under the CCPA.

11Security

Mutating endpoints require a bearer token configured by the operator and compared in constant time. There is no account system, no password storage, and no PII vault. We use standard transport security (HTTPS) when the dashboard is deployed behind a TLS-terminating proxy. No system is perfectly secure; you use the service at your own risk.

12Changes

We may revise this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use after a revision constitutes acceptance.

13Contact

For privacy questions, CCPA requests, or data-deletion requests, contact the operator listed in the dashboard's deployment configuration. California residents may also lodge a complaint with the California Privacy Protection Agency or the California Attorney General's Office.
